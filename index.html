Cubomb — Cute Cube Blast Puzzle

A colorful match puzzle full of cute cube characters, with endless levels. Swipe cubes, connect 3 or more of the same color, and build giant combos with rockets, bombs, and disco balls. Every level is generated for each player: a new board shape, a new color theme, varied goals, and surprise rules. I redesigned the game from scratch for version 2.0; it's on the App Store and Google Play for phones and tablets, in English and Turkish.

Personal tool

YouTube Content Automation

A system I built for my own use that prepares videos end to end for different YouTube channels. It generates channel-specific topics, writes the content, voices it, adds subtitles, and prepares the background images and footage. It reviews each channel's analytics through APIs, spots what needs to change, and shapes the next videos accordingly. It is actively running on channels with thousands of views.
